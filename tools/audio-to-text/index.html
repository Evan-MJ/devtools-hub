How to Convert Audio to Text

  1. Select your language from the dropdown
  2. Click "Start Recording" and allow microphone access
  3. Start speaking — your words appear in real-time
  4. Click "Stop Recording" when finished
  5. Copy or download your text

Common Use Cases

  • Students: Transcribe lectures and study notes
  • Journalists: Convert interview recordings to text
  • Professionals: Dictate emails, reports, meeting notes
  • Content Creators: Generate captions and transcripts
  • Accessibility: Voice typing for those with typing difficulties

Browser Compatibility

Uses the Web Speech API, supported in Chrome, Edge, and Safari. For best experience use Chrome or Edge.

FAQs

Is my audio sent to a server?

No. All speech recognition happens in your browser. Your audio never leaves your device.

How accurate is it?

90-95% for clear speech in quiet environments with a good microphone. Accuracy varies by language and background noise.

Is there a time limit?

No. You can speak for as long as you need. The browser transcribes continuously until you stop.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome, Edge, and Safari. Firefox has partial support.
