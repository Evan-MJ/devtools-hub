What is an IP Address?

An IP address (Internet Protocol address) is a unique numerical label assigned to each device connected to a computer network. Your public IP address is assigned by your Internet Service Provider (ISP) and can be used to identify your approximate location and ISP information.

How It Works

When you visit this page, your browser makes a request to external services to detect your public IP address. We then query geolocation databases to retrieve information about your IP, including country, region, city, ISP, and timezone. All processing happens in your browser - we don't log or store your IP address.

What Information is Revealed?

IP Address: Your unique public IP address visible to websites.

Geolocation: Approximate physical location based on IP ranges (typically accurate to city level).

ISP: Your Internet Service Provider's name and autonomous system number.

Coordinates: Latitude and longitude of your location (approximate, not precise).

Privacy Considerations

Your IP address alone doesn't reveal personal information like your name or exact address. However, it's important to be aware that websites can determine your approximate location and ISP from your IP. If you need more privacy, consider using a VPN service.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between public and private IP? A public IP address is assigned by your ISP and is visible to the internet. A private IP address is assigned by your local router for devices within your home network. This tool shows your public IP address.
Can someone find my exact location from my IP? No, IP addresses typically only reveal your approximate location (city level), not your exact address. Law enforcement or ISPs could potentially trace further with legal authority, but not regular websites.
Why is my location sometimes wrong? IP geolocation databases aren't always up to date. Your ISP might have allocated IP addresses in a different region than where you're actually located. VPN services can also make your location appear different.
Can I hide my IP address? Yes, you can use a VPN (Virtual Private Network) or Tor browser to hide your real IP address. This will make your location appear different to websites.
Is it safe to share my IP address? Sharing your public IP address is generally safe - it's what websites see every time you visit them. However, be cautious about sharing it in combination with other personal information.
