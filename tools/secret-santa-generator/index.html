How the Secret Santa Generator Works

Enter your names, run the draw, read the results. The generator takes the names you enter, treats every keep-apart rule as a hard constraint and produces an assignment where each person gives one gift and receives exactly one. Two people who are excluded from each other will never be paired in either direction. The shuffle is seeded by the browser's Web Crypto API, so the result is genuinely unpredictable rather than a pattern the eye can spot.

Before it draws, the tool checks whether a valid assignment exists. too many pairs in a small group, or a person's only possible matches are all ruled out, the draw stops and tells you which name is stuck instead of quietly breaking a rule. Party Reveal mode keeps every pairing hidden until each person taps their own name on the shared screen, which mirrors the classic hat draw for groups sitting together. Full List mode hands the organizer everything at once for pasting into a chat or printing.

From Feature Provider to Problem Solver

Most Secret Santa tools are invitation machines. They collect emails, create accounts, store your group on a server and hand out private links that expire or can be opened by the wrong person. That machinery earns its keep for a 200-person company draw scheduled three weeks out. It is dead weight when your family of eight is standing in the same living room and wants names right now.

This tool inverts that tradeoff. There is no server step at all: your names, your exclusions and the result live in your browser and nowhere else, so the draw finishes in one click and works even if the wifi is down. What an AI chatbot cannot do here is hold a private tap-to-reveal ceremony on a shared screen, enforce every exclusion pair under a fairness guarantee, and hand you a formatted list and printable cards in the same breath. That ceremony is the actual job, and it is what this page is built to do.

  • No email, no accounts, no links: nobody waits for an invitation or creates a password to learn who they are buying for.
  • Nothing stored online: names and results never leave the browser, so there is no server copy to leak and no link for a stranger to open.
  • Fairness with proof: exclusions are hard constraints, never a soft suggestion, and an impossible draw is reported honestly instead of silently bent.
  • Party mode: each person taps their own name to see their assignment privately, the way a hat draw works in the room.
  • Ready-to-share output: copy the whole list for WhatsApp, or print name cards for the fridge.
  • Redraw in one click: a latecomer joins, you add the name and redraw, and every exclusion still holds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Secret Santa generator really free?

Yes, completely free with no signup, no account, no ads and no limit on group size. The draw runs entirely in your browser, so there is nothing to pay for and nothing to subscribe to.

How do exclusion rules work for couples?

Put any two names on the keep-apart list and they will never draw each other in either direction. One rule covers both people, so a couple only needs a single entry. You can add as many pairs as you need, and the draw only succeeds when every rule can be honored.

Do I need an email address or an account?

No email is required, nobody registers and no invitation links are generated. Enter the names, run the draw and share the results yourself, which keeps the whole exchange off other people's servers.

How do I share the results with the group on WhatsApp?

Open the full list view and hit Copy List. Every assignment lands on your clipboard as plain text, one line per person, so you can paste the lot into WhatsApp, Messenger or Slack.

What happens if the draw is impossible?

The generator tells you instead of quietly breaking a rule. If a person has nobody left to give to, usually because of too many exclusion pairs in a small group, you get a clear message naming the problem so you can remove a pair or add another name.

Can I redraw if someone joins late?

Add the latecomer to the names list and press Draw Names again for a fresh assignment. The exclusions you set stay in place, and nobody needs to be notified through an app because you reshare the updated list yourself.

Where is my data stored?

Data lives in your browser's localStorage on your own device. The names, exclusions and results never leave the page, there is no database and no server copy, and the Clear All button wipes everything immediately.

Why is a local draw better than a link-based Secret Santa app?

Link-based tools keep your group on a server and hand out private links that can expire or be opened by the wrong person. A local draw hands you the whole result at once, so you can announce it in the room, print cards or paste it into the group chat, and there is no link for a stranger to find.
