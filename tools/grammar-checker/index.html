How Our Grammar Checker Works

Unlike other grammar checkers, we don't just correct your text - we explain why it's wrong. This helps you learn grammar rules, not just copy corrections.

  • Error Detection: Identifies grammar, spelling, and punctuation errors
  • Explanation: Explains why each error is wrong
  • Correction: Shows the corrected version
  • Rule Learning: Helps you understand grammar rules

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is this better than Grammarly?

Our tool explains WHY your grammar is wrong, not just what to fix. This helps you learn and remember grammar rules for future writing.

Is this grammar checker free?

Yes, completely free with no signup required. We believe everyone should have access to grammar learning tools.

What types of errors can it detect?

It can detect subject-verb agreement, verb tense, articles (a/an/the), preposition usage, word order, punctuation, and more.

Can it check non-English text?

Currently optimized for English text. Results may vary for other languages.
