Frequently Asked Questions

Is my timing data stored anywhere?

No. All timing happens in your browser. When you close the page, all data is deleted. We don't use cookies or tracking.

Will the timer work if I switch tabs?

Yes. The timer continues running in the background. However, for best results keep the tab open. Audio alerts will still sound even if you're on another tab.

What's the Pomodoro Technique?

It's a time management method: work for 25 minutes, take a 5-minute break, repeat. After 4 sessions, take a longer 15-minute break. This structured approach helps maintain focus and prevents burnout.

What's Tabata/HIIT?

Tabata is a type of High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT): 20 seconds of all-out exercise, 10 seconds rest, repeated 8 times (4 minutes total). Our interval timer is fully customizable for any HIIT workout.
