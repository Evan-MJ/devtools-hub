What Day Is It Right Now?

This page answers the simple question: "what day is it?" Whether you need to know today's date, the day of the week, what day a future date will fall on, or how many days are between two dates, this free tool gives you instant answers.

What This Tool Does

  • Today's Date in 8 formats (ISO, US, European, Chinese, Japanese, Unix, etc.)
  • Day of Year — what number day of the year is it (1-365/366)
  • Week of Year — ISO week number (1-53)
  • Days Until Weekend — how many days until Saturday
  • Days Left in Year — countdown to January 1st
  • Year Progress — what percentage of the year is complete
  • Date Difference — calculate days/weeks/months/years between two dates
  • Add/Subtract Days — find a date N days from a given date
  • Day of Week Finder — what day was or will be a specific date
  • Monthly Calendar — visual calendar with today highlighted
  • Upcoming Holidays — countdown to US holidays

How It Works

All date calculations run locally in your browser using the JavaScript Date object and Intl.DateTimeFormat API. No data is sent to any server. The current time comes from your device's system clock, which is typically synchronized with NTP servers to within milliseconds.

Why "What Day Is It" Matters

The question "what day is it" is one of the most googled questions in the world, with over 4.8 million searches per month globally. People ask it when they wake up disoriented, are scheduling across timezones, or need a quick reference for forms and deadlines.

Privacy

All calculations happen 100% in your browser. We don't log your queries, store your dates, or track which dates you check. The page works offline once loaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

What day is it today?

The current day of the week is displayed at the top of this page, refreshed in real time. Today is shown in 8 different date formats.

What is today's date?

Today's date appears at the top of the page in multiple formats: ISO 8601 (e.g., 2026-06-02), US (06/02/2026), European (02/06/2026), Chinese (2026年6月2日), Japanese (2026年6月2日), and Unix timestamp.

How do I calculate days between two dates?

Use the "Date Difference Calculator" below. Enter a start date and an end date, then click Calculate. The result shows the total number of days, plus equivalent values in weeks, months, years, hours, and minutes.

How do I add days to a date?

Use the "Add or Subtract Days" calculator. Enter a starting date and the number of days (positive to add, negative to subtract). The result shows the new date and the day of the week it will be.

What day of the week was a specific date?

Use the "Day of Week Finder" — pick any past or future date and the tool instantly tells you the day of the week. It also shows how many days ago or in the future that date is.

How accurate is this tool?

Calculations use JavaScript's built-in Date object, which is accurate to the millisecond for any date between 100,000 BC and 100,000 AD. The displayed current time comes from your device clock, synced via NTP.

Does this work offline?

Yes. Once the page loads, all features work without an internet connection. The page makes no API calls during use.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. No data leaves your browser. We don't log which dates you check or store any information.
