Daily Wordle

How to Play Wordle

Guess the daily 5-letter word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word. After each guess, the color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was to the word.

  • Green = The letter is in the correct position.
  • Yellow = The letter is in the word but in the wrong position.
  • Gray = The letter is not in the word at all.

Why This Beats AI for Wordle

AI chatbots cannot play Wordle interactively. They cannot maintain game state, track letter positions across guesses, or provide the real-time satisfaction of cracking a daily puzzle with your own brain. This tool is pure client-side JavaScript - no AI, no server calls, no tracking. Your streak is saved only in your browser.

Daily Challenge

Every player gets the same word each day, determined by the date. Can you solve it? Come back tomorrow for a new challenge. Your statistics and streak are preserved across visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Wordle game free?

Yes, 100% free. No signup, no ads, no tracking. Play as much as you want.

How does the daily word work?

The daily word is generated from the date. Every player worldwide gets the same word each day. A new word appears at midnight UTC.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, the game is fully responsive. Tap the on-screen keyboard or use your physical keyboard.

Are my stats saved?

Yes, your game statistics and streak are saved in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data will reset them.

Can I practice more than once per day?

The daily word is one puzzle per day. But you can start a new game anytime to play with random words for practice.
