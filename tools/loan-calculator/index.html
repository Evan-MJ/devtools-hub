Frequently Asked Questions

How is monthly payment calculated?

Monthly payment = P * [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1] where P is principal, r is monthly interest rate, n is total months. This includes principal and interest only - property tax and insurance are separate.

Does the extra payment calculator include PMI?

PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance) is typically required when down payment is less than 20%. Our calculator estimates when you'll reach 20% equity based on your home's original value and standard 3% annual appreciation rate.

Is my data saved?

No. All calculations happen in your browser. Your loan details never leave your device.
