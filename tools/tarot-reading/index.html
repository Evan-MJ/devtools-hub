How Tarot Reading Works

This tool draws from a complete 78-card Rider-Waite-Smith deck - 22 Major Arcana (life's karmic and spiritual lessons) and 56 Minor Arcana across four suits (Wands = passion and action, Cups = emotions and relationships, Swords = intellect and conflict, Pentacles = material and money). Each draw is deterministic: a Mulberry32 pseudo-random number generator is seeded from your birthdate, today's date, the spread type, and a hash of your question text. Same seed, same cards, every time - reproducible across sessions and across devices.

What is a Birth Card and how do I find mine?

Your Birth Card is the Major Arcana card associated with your birthdate, calculated by reducing all digits of your birth month + day + year sum to a single digit 0-21 (with 22 as The Fool). For example, June 15, 1990 has digit sum 31 -> 3+1 = 4 = The Emperor. Our tool computes it deterministically.

What do the Major Arcana cards mean?

The 22 Major Arcana represent life's spiritual lessons: The Fool, The Magician, The High Priestess, The Empress, The Emperor, The Hierophant, The Lovers, The Chariot, Strength, The Hermit, Wheel of Fortune, Justice, The Hanged Man, Death, Temperance, The Devil, The Tower, The Star, The Moon, The Sun, Judgement, and The World. Each has full symbolism, upright meanings, and reversed meanings.

How are the Minor Arcana cards divided?

56 Minor Arcana cards divided into 4 suits of 14 each: Wands (Fire, passion/action), Cups (Water, emotions/relationships), Swords (Air, intellect/conflict), Pentacles (Earth, material/money). Each suit has 10 numbered cards and 4 Court Cards (Page, Knight, Queen, King).
