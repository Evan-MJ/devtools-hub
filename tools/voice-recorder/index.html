Frequently Asked Questions

How does the online voice recorder work?

This tool uses your browser's built-in MediaRecorder API and Web Audio API. When you click Record, your browser captures audio from your microphone entirely on your device. The audio is processed locally and is ready for playback or download as soon as you stop. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Is this voice recorder really free?

Yes, completely free with no limits, no watermark, no signup, and no hidden fees. Record as many times as you want for as long as you want.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Works on Chrome for Android and Safari on iOS. On iPhone the site must be served over HTTPS. Grant microphone permission when prompted.

What is the difference between MP3 and WAV?

MP3 is compressed and smaller - ideal for sharing via email, messaging, or uploading to platforms. WAV is uncompressed lossless audio - much larger file size but preserves every detail, best for editing in audio software.

Can I record system audio?

No, this tool records from your microphone only. Browser security rules do not allow websites to capture system audio directly. For system audio recording, use a desktop application like OBS Studio.

How long can I record for?

There is no hard time limit. Recording uses your device's memory. Most modern devices handle 1-2 hours without issues. For very long recordings, save periodically to avoid losing audio if the tab crashes.
