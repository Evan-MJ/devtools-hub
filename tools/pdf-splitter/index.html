Problem: Most PDF Splitters Upload Your Documents

When you split a PDF online, your tax returns, contracts, and personal documents are typically uploaded to a remote server. Your private files pass through unknown infrastructure.

How We Solve This

  • Zero Upload: Your PDF never leaves your device. All processing happens in your browser
  • Three Split Modes: Extract every page, specific ranges, or fixed chunks
  • Page Range Format: Use simple syntax like "1-3, 5, 7-10" to select exactly which pages you want
  • Batch Download: Download all split files at once or individually
  • No File Size Limit: Split files up to 100 MB or more

Why AI Cannot Replace This Tool

AI chatbots can describe how to split a PDF, but they cannot actually split your file. PDF manipulation requires reading binary file structures, copying page trees, and generating valid PDF output. This tool performs all operations precisely on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my PDF uploaded to any server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser using the pdf-lib library. Your file never leaves your device.

What is page range syntax?

Use commas to separate pages or ranges: "1-3" extracts pages 1 through 3, "5" extracts page 5, "1-3, 5, 7-10" extracts all those pages into one PDF.

Is there a file size limit?

The tool works entirely in your browser. Very large PDFs (100+ MB) may be slower but will still work.

Does splitting reduce quality?

No. Pages are copied without re-encoding. The content quality remains exactly the same as the original.
