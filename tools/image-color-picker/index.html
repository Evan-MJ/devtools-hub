What is an Image Color Picker?

An image color picker (also called a photo color picker or color extractor) lets you upload any image and click on individual pixels to identify their exact color values. Unlike dominant color extractors that show only the most common colors, this tool gives you pixel-level precision. You can inspect any area of a photo, screenshot, or design mockup and get the exact HEX, RGB, HSL, and CMYK codes ready to use in your projects.

How It Works

Upload your image by dragging and dropping, clicking to browse, or pressing Ctrl+V to paste from clipboard. The image loads entirely in your browser. Move your cursor over the image to see a magnified view with a crosshair showing the exact pixel under inspection. Click to lock that color. All four color format values appear instantly, ready to copy with a single click. Every color you pick is saved to the history bar for quick reference.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from a dominant color extractor?

A dominant color extractor analyzes the whole image and returns the most common colors. An image color picker lets you click on any specific pixel to get its exact color. Use the extractor for quick palettes and the picker when you need precise colors from specific areas.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your image data never leaves your device. You can verify this by opening your browser's developer tools and checking the Network tab — you will see zero outbound requests containing image data.

What image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, and most common image formats. Maximum file size is 20MB. For best results, use high-resolution images for accurate pixel sampling.

Can I paste a screenshot directly?

Yes. Press Ctrl+V (or Cmd+V on Mac) anywhere on the page after taking a screenshot. The image will be loaded directly from your clipboard. This works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and most modern browsers.

Why does the magnifier show a zoomed view?

The 8x magnifier makes it possible to pick colors from tiny details or thin lines that would be hard to click precisely at normal zoom. The crosshair helps you identify the exact pixel being sampled.

Can I copy all picked colors at once?

Click any color swatch in the history bar to re-select it. Each individual color format (HEX, RGB, HSL, CMYK) can be copied with a single click. A confirmation message appears briefly when the value is copied.
