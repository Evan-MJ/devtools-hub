Why can't AI image generators create a proper logo?

AI image generators create raster images that are resolution-fixed. A logo must be a vector graphic to scale infinitely without quality loss. AI also cannot reliably render precise text (often produces garbled characters) or create clean, editable SVG files.

What is the difference between PNG and SVG for logos?

PNG is a raster image format best for web use — it has a fixed resolution and transparent background support. SVG is a vector format that scales infinitely without quality loss, making it ideal for printing on business cards, billboards, and merchandise. For professional use, always keep your SVG master file.
