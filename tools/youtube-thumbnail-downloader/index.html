Frequently Asked Questions

Is this YouTube thumbnail downloader really free?

Yes, 100% free. No signup, no premium tier, no daily limit, no watermark. The tool fetches thumbnails directly from Google's public CDN which requires no API key.

What resolutions are available?

YouTube generates up to 5 thumbnail sizes: maxresdefault (1280x720, HD), sddefault (640x480), hqdefault (480x360), mqdefault (320x180), and default (120x90). Not all videos have all sizes — older or SD videos may lack maxresdefault. The tool automatically detects which are available.

Does it work with private videos?

Unlisted videos (accessible via link) work fine. Private videos return a generic placeholder thumbnail. Deleted videos return a "video unavailable" image.

Can I use downloaded thumbnails?

YouTube thumbnails are owned by the video creator. Use them for personal reference, design inspiration, or fair-use purposes. Redistribution may require permission from the copyright holder.

Do you store my searches?

No. All processing happens in your browser. No search data is sent to any server. Your video URLs are not logged or stored.

Why is maxresdefault missing for some videos?

YouTube only generates the 1280x720 maxresdefault thumbnail for videos uploaded in HD (720p or higher). Videos older than ~2013 or uploaded in SD will not have this resolution. The tool shows "Not available" for these cases.
