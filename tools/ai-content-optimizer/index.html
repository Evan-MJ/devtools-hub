Frequently Asked Questions

                

                    
Is the AI content optimizer really free with no signup?

                    
Yes. 100% free, no signup, no daily cap, no paywall. You give the AI a topic or draft plus optional key points, tone and length, and it returns an answer-engine-friendly content package.

                

                

                    
What makes content AI-answer-friendly?

                    
Answer-first headings, direct-answer paragraphs, Q&A structure, FAQ coverage and factual-but-hedged claims. These are signals AI Overviews and ChatGPT tend to cite.

No. It produces an optimized draft package; human review for facts, brand voice and legal claims is still required.
