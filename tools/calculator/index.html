Frequently Asked Questions

Is this calculator free to use? Yes, completely free. No signup, no account required, no ads interrupting your work. Your calculations happen entirely in your browser.
How accurate are the calculations? We use JavaScript's native floating-point arithmetic, providing the same precision as professional calculators. Loan calculations use standard amortization formulas.
Can I use this on my phone? Yes, the calculator works on any device with a web browser. The interface adapts to mobile screens for touch input.
Is my data safe? All calculations happen locally in your browser. We don't transmit your numbers to any server. Close the page and your data is gone.
What is the difference between basic and scientific mode? Basic mode handles addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and percentages. Scientific mode adds trigonometric functions, logarithms, roots, and constants for advanced math.
How does the loan calculator work? It uses the standard amortization formula: M = P[r(1+r)^n]/[(1+r)^n-1] where M is monthly payment, P is principal, r is monthly interest rate, and n is total payments.
What BMI range is considered healthy? BMI below 18.5 is underweight, 18.5-24.9 is normal, 25-29.9 is overweight, and 30+ is obese. Note that BMI does not distinguish between muscle and fat.
