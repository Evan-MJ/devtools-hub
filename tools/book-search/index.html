How to Search Books

  1. Pick a search mode using the buttons above: Title, Author, ISBN, or All (combined)
  2. Type your query in the search box. For ISBN mode, type the 10 or 13 digit ISBN
  3. Click Search or press Enter. Results appear in under 2 seconds
  4. Click any result card to see full details, description, and links to buy or borrow from your library

Where to Find a Book After You Find It

Every result links to multiple services so you can choose:

  • Open Library - Free digital borrow (Internet Archive lending library, where available)
  • Google Books - Preview pages, find nearby libraries, see reviews
  • WorldCat - Search your local library catalog worldwide (the OCLC union catalog)
  • Amazon - Buy new or used (where available)
  • Open Library Page - Full bibliographic record, all editions, author bio

What AI Cannot Do Here

Large language models like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini regularly hallucinate book details: wrong ISBNs, wrong publishers, wrong page counts, and even books that do not exist. The ISBN database is the only authoritative source for what was actually printed, when, and by whom. This tool queries the live Open Library catalog in real time - no guessing, no fabrication, no login.
