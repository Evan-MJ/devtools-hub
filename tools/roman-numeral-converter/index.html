How Roman Numerals Work

Roman numerals use seven symbols: I (1), V (5), X (10), L (50), C (100), D (500), M (1000). Numbers are formed by combining these symbols using two rules:

  • Addition: When a larger symbol precedes a smaller one, add their values (VI = 5+1 = 6)
  • Subtraction: When a smaller symbol precedes a larger one, subtract it (IV = 5-1 = 4)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the largest number you can write in Roman numerals?

Using standard notation, the largest is 3,999 (MMMCMXCIX). For larger numbers, a vinculum (overline) multiplies the value by 1,000, allowing representation up to 3,999,999.

Why is 4 written as IV instead of IIII?

Roman numerals use the subtraction principle to avoid four consecutive identical symbols. IV is shorter and more standard, though IIII still appears on some clock faces for aesthetic symmetry.

How do I convert a date to Roman numerals for a tattoo?

Use our Date → Roman tab. Each part (month, day, year) is converted separately and joined with dots. For example, June 10, 2026 becomes VI·X·MMXXVI.

What is 2026 in Roman numerals?

2026 in Roman numerals is MMXXVI. M=1000, M=1000, X=10, X=10, V=5, I=1. Total: 2000+20+5+1 = 2026.
