What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is scrambled Latin placeholder text used in design, publishing, and web development since the 1500s. It has a natural letter distribution similar to English, making it ideal for mocking up layouts before real content is ready. Unlike AI-generated text, Lorem Ipsum is intentionally nonsensical — designers need visual fill, not readable copy.

Why Not Use AI for Placeholder Text?

AI chatbots generate coherent, meaningful sentences — exactly what you don't want in placeholder text. Meaningful text distracts reviewers and clients into reading content instead of evaluating layout, typography, and spacing. Lorem Ipsum is visual-only filler that keeps attention on design.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use the generated text in commercial projects?

Yes. Lorem Ipsum is derived from a public domain Latin text (Cicero's De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum, 45 BC). The scrambled version is not copyrightable. Use it freely in any project.

How is Lorem Ipsum different from random text?

Lorem Ipsum has a realistic letter frequency and word length distribution similar to English. Random text (like "asdf ghjk") doesn't provide an accurate preview of how real content will look in your layout.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely free with no signup, no limits, and no server calls. All processing happens in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.

Can I generate more than 100 paragraphs?

The interface limits to 100 per generation for performance, but you can generate multiple times. Each generation produces fresh randomized output from the same Lorem Ipsum corpus.
