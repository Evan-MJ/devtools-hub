Standard Business Card Sizes Worldwide

The default size in this tool is the US standard 3.5 x 2 inch (88.9 x 50.8 mm) at 300 DPI, which matches the most common print spec in North America and is accepted by most global print services. The European standard 85 x 55 mm is within 1mm of the US size so most designs transfer cleanly. For Japanese markets the 91 x 55 mm spec is common. The 300 DPI PNG export is high enough resolution for any of these sizes when scaled appropriately.

Tips for Designing a Great Business Card

  • Keep it readable — Body text should be at least 8pt at print size. Our default 11px on a 600px-tall canvas maps to roughly 11pt at 3.5 x 2 inch output.
  • Use high contrast — Dark text on light background is the safest combination. Reserve accent colors for borders, separators, or a single word.
  • Include the essentials — Name, title, company, phone, email, and website are the six fields that 95% of recipients expect. Address is optional in our modern remote-work world.
  • Pick one focal point — Your name should be the largest text element. Job title and company are secondary. Contact details are tertiary.
  • Test with vCard — Always export the vCard file and import it into your own phone to verify the contact information lands correctly.
