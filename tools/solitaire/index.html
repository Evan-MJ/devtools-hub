About Solitaire

Solitaire, also known as Klondike Solitaire or Patience, is the world's most popular single-player card game. The goal is to build four foundation piles from Ace to King, one for each suit, by moving cards between the seven tableau columns.

How to Play

  • Tableau: Build DOWN in alternating colors (red on black, black on red). A sequence of cards can be moved together.
  • Foundations: Build UP by suit from Ace to King. Click a card to send it to its foundation.
  • Stock: Click to draw cards. Draw 1 or 3 cards at a time.
  • Empty tableau: Only a King (or a King sequence) can be placed in an empty column.
  • Winning: Move all 52 cards to the foundation piles.

Why AI Can't Play Solitaire For You

Solitaire is an interactive game of skill and chance that requires real-time decision making, spatial reasoning, and physical interaction. AI language models cannot click cards, drag sequences, or experience the satisfaction of solving a tricky deal. This tool provides genuine interactive entertainment that AI cannot replicate.

What is the difference between Draw 1 and Draw 3?

Draw 1 deals one card at a time from the stock (easier). Draw 3 deals three cards at a time (more challenging).

Yes, unlimited undo is available. Click the Undo button to go back.

How do I win?
