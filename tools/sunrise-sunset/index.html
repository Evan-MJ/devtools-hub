How It Works

Enter any city name or use the quick select buttons to find today's sunrise and sunset times. Powered by free Open-Meteo API - no API key required.

Why is this useful?

Knowing exact sunrise and sunset times is essential for photography (golden hour), outdoor activities, gardening, and planning your day around natural light.

What is day length?

Day length is the time between sunrise and sunset - the number of hours of daylight you get.
