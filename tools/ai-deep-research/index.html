What Is an AI Deep Research Tool?

An AI deep research tool takes a single question and turns it into a cited research report. Instead of answering from memory, it plans a search, browses many sources, reads the most relevant ones, and synthesizes a structured answer. It is built for questions that need evidence: market trends, technical comparisons, policy shifts, scientific topics, competitor analysis.

How This Tool Researches

Deep mode

Searches the live web with broad coverage, reads the most relevant sources in depth, then synthesizes a full report. Best for complex questions.

Quick mode

Runs a focused web search and synthesizes from the top sources. Best for fast answers and follow-up checks.

Why Cite Sources?

A cited report lets you verify claims instead of trusting a single generated paragraph. Every claim in the report is anchored to a source you can open. This matters because general AI answers can still be wrong, and a cited report is checkable rather than a black box.

How It Uses AI

The research pipeline uses the site's AI provider chain (Groq, NVIDIA, Cloudflare Workers AI, Gemini) with automatic fallback, and post-processes long reports through the DeAI humanizer before display. If the humanizer is unavailable, the raw report is shown unchanged.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between deep and quick research?

Deep research searches with broad coverage and reads the most relevant sources before synthesizing. Quick research runs a tighter search and synthesizes from the top sources. Use deep for complex questions, quick for fast checks.

Does the tool store or share my question?

The question is sent to the site's own AI backend to run the research and is not retained for reuse. Like the other AI tools on this site, your input is processed by a third-party AI provider and is not used for training.

How are sources chosen?

The research backend ranks sources by relevance to the question and returns the top results with their content. The synthesis step cites only the sources it actually used.

Can I trust the report?

The report is a synthesis, not a primary source. Verify important claims against the cited sources. Use quick mode to check a specific fact quickly.

Does the humanizer change the meaning?

The DeAI humanizer rewrites long prose to reduce AI-writing patterns while preserving meaning, using a multi-stage pipeline with a quality gate. If it declines, the original report is returned.
