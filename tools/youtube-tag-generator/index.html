    How to use YouTube tags effectively

    YouTube tags are descriptive keywords you add to a video in YouTube Studio under "Show more" → "Tags". While YouTube has stated tags are not a major ranking signal, they help with three things: (1) fixing common misspellings of your topic, (2) clarifying context for borderline content, and (3) surfacing your video for related searches on YouTube and Google. Combine this tool with a strong title and compelling thumbnail for best results.

    Will these tags help my video rank?

    YouTube officially states that tags play a minimal role in video discovery compared to title, thumbnail, and description. Use this tool to generate tags that complement a strong title and compelling thumbnail.
