Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from Google Translate?

Our AI provides context-aware translations that understand idioms, slang, and natural phrasing. Simple translators often produce awkward literal translations, while ours captures the intended meaning.

Is this translator free?

Yes, completely free with no signup required. No hidden fees, no premium tier.

What languages are supported?

We support 38 languages including English, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi, Portuguese, Russian, and many more.

Is my text private?

Your text is sent to our AI for translation and is not stored or logged. No data is retained after translation.

How accurate is the translation?

Our AI model provides high-quality translations with context understanding. For critical documents like legal or medical texts, we recommend human review.

You can translate up to 5,000 characters at a time. For longer texts, break them into smaller segments.
