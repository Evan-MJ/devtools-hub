Frequently Asked Questions

Is this prompt library really free with no signup?

Yes. 100% free, no signup, no account, no email, no daily quota, no watermark. All 88 templates are embedded inline as JavaScript constants - they load once and work offline.

Why use this instead of ChatGPT or other AI chatbots?

AI chatbots are great at generating ONE prompt, but they cannot: (1) show you 88 curated prompts across 8 different platforms side-by-side, (2) auto-extract {{variable}} placeholders and generate input forms, (3) live-test a prompt against a live model in the same tab, (4) rewrite your prompt in Midjourney, DALL-E, or Stable Diffusion syntax, (5) remember your favorites across sessions.

What AI platforms are supported?

Eight platforms: ChatGPT (GPT-4o/o1 style), Claude (Sonnet 4.5 style), Midjourney (v6 syntax with --ar, --s, --v flags), DALL-E 3 (natural language style), Stable Diffusion (SDXL prompt weighting), Google Gemini (2.0 Flash style), Llama (Meta's open-weight Llama 3 family), and Microsoft Copilot.

What is the Live Test Sandbox?

Click 'Test in Sandbox' on any template to send it to our Groq-hosted model, with automatic NVIDIA and Gemini fallback, and see the output instantly in a side panel. The sandbox uses the same prompt formatting as your target platform (e.g., Midjourney prompts are sent with their --ar flags preserved).

What does the Cross-Platform Converter do?

Paste any prompt into the converter, and it rewrites the prompt in the style and syntax of each of 8 AI platforms. For example, "a cat on a roof" becomes a Midjourney-style "a cat on a moss-covered tile roof, golden hour, cinematic lighting --ar 16:9 --v 6", a DALL-E-style natural language description, and an SDXL prompt with weighted parentheses.

Are my prompts and favorites private?

Yes. Favorites live in your browser's localStorage. Prompts you test in the sandbox are sent to our Groq endpoint for inference and not stored or logged.

Can I export my prompts?

Yes. Each prompt can be copied individually, or you can export your entire favorites list as TXT, Markdown, or JSON for backup or sharing.

Does this work offline?

All 88 templates and the variable form generator work fully offline once the page loads. The Live Test Sandbox and Cross-Platform Converter require internet to call the AI provider.

What if I want a template that is not listed?

Use the Prompt Improver: paste your draft, and the AI will rewrite it in 3 improved variants optimized for general LLM use. You can also use the Cross-Platform Converter on any prompt text.
