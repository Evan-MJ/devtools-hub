How to Cut Audio Online

  1. Upload your audio file by clicking or dragging it into the dropzone
  2. Set trim points by adjusting start and end times, or click on the waveform
  3. Preview the selected portion to make sure it is right
  4. Click "Trim Audio" to process and download your trimmed audio

Features

Waveform Visualization

See your audio as a visual waveform. Click to set precise start and end points.

Real-time Preview

Preview the trimmed section before processing. Make sure it is exactly what you want.

100% Private

All processing happens in your browser. Your audio files never leave your device.

Multiple Formats

Support for MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, OGG, AAC and more. Export to your preferred format.
