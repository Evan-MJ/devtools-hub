FAQs

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens in your browser. Your video never leaves your device.

What video formats are supported?

Any format your browser can play: MP4 (H.264), WebM (VP8/VP9), MOV, AVI, MKV.

What is the output quality?

Opus codec at 192 kbps — excellent quality comparable to MP3 320 kbps.

Is there a file size limit?

No hard limit. Very large files limited by browser memory.
