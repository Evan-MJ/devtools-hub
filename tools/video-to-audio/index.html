Frequently Asked Questions

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens in your browser using the Web Audio API. Your video never leaves your device.

What video formats are supported?

Any format your browser can play: MP4, WebM, MOV, AVI, MKV, FLV. Chrome and Edge support the widest range of formats.

What is the output audio quality?

Opus codec at 192 kbps — excellent quality comparable to MP3 at 320 kbps. Perfect for music, podcasts, and voice recordings.

Is there a file size limit?

No hard limit, but very large files may be limited by your browser's memory. 2GB should work on modern devices.

Can I extract audio from YouTube videos?

No. This tool works with local video files you upload. Use a dedicated YouTube downloader for online videos.
