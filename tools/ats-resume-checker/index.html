    What the score actually measures

    The score is a comparison against the job description you pasted, not against the market. Change the posting and the same resume gets a different score — that is by design, because the ATS compares against that one posting too.

    • Read the per-dimension breakdown rather than the total: keyword coverage, hard-skill match, structure, quantified impact and formatting are weighted into one number, and the weakest dimension is the one to fix.
    • Most rejections at this stage are keyword coverage. A skill described in your own words is invisible to a keyword matcher, so mirror the wording of the posting for the skills you actually have.
    • Quantified impact is where most resumes lose points: "managed a team" reads far worse than "managed 6 engineers and cut deployment time by 40%".
    • The formatting warnings are about machine readability — section headings, date formats, tables and columns — not about aesthetics.
    • Treat the suggestions as a checklist of edits to make, not as text to paste in. An ATS screen is not a human reader; your resume still has to read well to the person who opens it.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is a good ATS score?

    Jobscan recommends 75% as the target. However, 65% is often realistic and still gets interviews. Scores above 80% may indicate over-optimization - stuffing every keyword from the job description into your resume. Aim for 65-80% with the missing keywords list as your guide.

    Why does my score change between resumes and job descriptions?

    The score is a measure of how well your resume matches a specific job description. A 90% match for a marketing role might be a 40% match for a data analyst role. This is exactly how real ATS work - they compare your resume against the specific job's keyword list. Always tailor your resume to the job you are applying for.

    Does the AI suggestions feature upload my resume?

    Yes, but only when you click the button. The AI suggestions call sends your resume and job description to our AI provider (Groq, with NVIDIA and Gemini fallback) for personalized improvement tips. We do not store or log your resume. You can still get a useful ATS score, keyword analysis, and format check without using AI suggestions - those are 100% client-side.

    What if my resume has tables, columns, or special characters?

    Real ATS systems struggle to parse tables, columns, text boxes, headers/footers, images, and special characters. The format check section of our tool warns you about these issues. We recommend using a single-column, plain-text format with standard section headings (Experience, Education, Skills) and no images. Save as .docx if the employer accepts it.

    Can I use this for cover letters too?

    The tool is designed for resumes, but the same keyword-matching logic works for cover letters. Paste your cover letter as the "resume" and the job description as the "job description" to see how well your cover letter addresses the role. However, cover letter scoring is less standardized - recruiters read cover letters more freely than ATS scans them.
