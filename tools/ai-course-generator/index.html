Frequently Asked Questions

Is this AI course generator really free?

Yes. 100% free with no signup, no daily limits, no hidden costs. Generate complete course designs without creating an account.

What does the generated course include?

The course design includes title, description, learning objectives, modules, lessons, key points, quiz questions with answers, estimated total duration and quiz pass criteria.

How does it differ from asking ChatGPT for a course outline?

ChatGPT can draft modules, but it does not design a complete sequenced course with measurable objectives, lesson-level quizzes and pass criteria in one structured output.

Does it use AI humanization on the generated text?

Yes. Long text fields such as the course description and quiz answers pass through the DeAI humanizer when configured; if the DeAI service is unavailable, the raw AI output is used.

Is the generated content factually safe?

The generator instructs the model to avoid fabricating facts about real people, companies or events. It is a drafting aid and human review is required before use in formal education settings.
