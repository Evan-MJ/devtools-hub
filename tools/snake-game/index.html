About Snake Game

Snake is the classic arcade game that has entertained generations. Control a snake as it moves around the board, eat food to grow longer, and avoid running into walls or your own tail. Simple to learn but challenging to master.

How to Play

  • Controls: Use Arrow Keys, WASD, or the on-screen D-pad to change direction
  • Goal: Eat as much food as possible without hitting walls or yourself
  • Scoring: Each food item gives 10 points. Gold food gives 50 points but briefly appears
  • Speed: Choose from Easy, Medium, Hard, or Insane for different challenge levels
  • Grid: Small (15x15), Medium (20x20), or Large (25x25) play area
  • Wall Wrap: Enable wrap mode to pass through walls and appear on the opposite side
  • Pause: Press Space or P to pause/resume at any time

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this snake game free?

Yes, completely free. No signup, no ads, no hidden costs. Play as much as you want.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, the game includes touch-friendly D-pad controls and works on all screen sizes.

What is wall wrap mode?

When enabled, your snake passes through walls and appears on the opposite side instead of dying. This makes the game easier and creates interesting movement patterns.

How is the high score saved?

Your high score is saved in your browser's local storage. It persists between sessions on the same device and browser.

What happens when I hit myself?

If your snake's head touches its own body, the game ends. Plan your moves carefully and leave yourself room to turn.

Can I change settings during a game?

Speed and grid settings only apply when starting a new game. You can toggle wall wrap mode during gameplay.
