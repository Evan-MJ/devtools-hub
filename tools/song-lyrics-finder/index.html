Frequently Asked Questions

                

                    

                        Is this song lyrics finder really free?
                        
Yes, 100% free. No signup, no premium tier, no daily limit. The lyrics service is server-side and requires no API key on your end.

                    

                    

                        Do you store my search history?
                        
Search history is stored only in your browser's localStorage. It is never sent to our servers. You can clear it anytime by clicking the X on any history chip, or by clearing your browser data. The lyrics themselves are fetched by our server, which keeps upstream out of your browser entirely.

                    

                    

                        Why is the song I searched for not found?
                        
The lyrics database covers millions of songs but not every single one, and very new or non-English releases may not be there yet. In that case we provide three direct deep links to the largest lyrics databases in the world (Genius, AZLyrics, Musixmatch) so you can find the song elsewhere.

                    

                    

                        Can I download the lyrics?
                        
Yes. Click the "Download .txt" button after viewing lyrics. A text file containing the artist, title, and full lyrics will be saved to your device.

                    

                    

                        How is this different from Google search for lyrics?
                        
Google often shows lyrics snippets inside a card with ads and "Sign in" prompts. This tool shows the full lyrics in one clean view with no ads, no tracking pixels, and no sign-in walls. Faster and more private.
