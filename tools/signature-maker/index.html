Problem: Signing a Document Requires Premium Tools

Every major tool that puts a signature on a PDF pushes you into a paid plan. DocuSign limits free accounts to a couple of documents before asking for a subscription. Smallpdf offers only a handful of free tasks per day and then adds its watermark. iLovePDF restricts free outputs with watermarks. Adobe Acrobat, the classic option, needs a subscription around $24.99 a month. People who just need to sign a single form, a contract, a permission slip, or a freelance agreement end up paying, registering, and fighting daily caps.

Why AI Chatbots Cannot Sign Your PDF

When you ask a chatbot to sign a document you get advice, not a file. Handwriting generation needs a drawing surface, a signature photo needs pixel-level background removal, and embedding a custom image into a PDF requires parsing the document structure and writing binary image commands into each page. Free LLMs cannot touch your local files, draw a freehand stroke, or produce a modified PDF, so they have no way to place your real signature onto a real contract. This tool does exactly that in your browser.

How to Use in Four Steps

  1. Draw, type, or upload your signature.
  2. Optionally download it as a transparent PNG.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this really free?

Yes, every part is free with no signup and no watermark. DocuSign caps free documents, SmallC caps a few tasks per day, iLovePDF adds watermarks, and Adobe charges by subscription. Here the limits are lifted.

Do my files get uploaded?

No. The Canvas API reads your signature and pdf-lib builds the finished PDF completely in your browser. Nothing is transmitted. The Network tab will show zero requests carrying your documents.

Can I use my existing paper signature?

Yes. Photograph it, upload the photo, and use the removal slider to erase the background. You will have a clean transparent signature you can place onto any PDF.

What if my signature is too small?

Use the Signature Size slider while the page is open. It scales the signature image before it is written into the PDF, so the result stays sharp.

Will my signature look right on the PDF?

Yes. The signature shown in the preview is placed on the page at the exact coordinates you choose. You see it positioned before you press Sign, so you control exactly how the final file looks.

Is this a legal electronic signature?

It is an electronic signature you place yourself, which satisfies the visual signature need on many documents. Regulated documents that need audit trails or timestamp verification should use a dedicated e-signature platform, but for most people this signature image is exactly what they need.

Which browsers work?

Any current Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari browser. The tool uses the standard Canvas, File, and PDF APIs that every modern browser ships with.

Do you keep my signature?

No. Nothing is stored. Close the tab and your signature and PDF are gone. There are no accounts, no tracking cookies, and no upload at all.
