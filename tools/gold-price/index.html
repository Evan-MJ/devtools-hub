Frequently Asked Questions

What is XAU?

XAU is the ISO 4217 standard code for one troy ounce of gold. XAU/USD represents the price of one troy ounce of gold in US Dollars.

How often is the price updated?

The price updates every 60 seconds automatically. You can also click the Refresh button for an immediate update.

Is this data reliable?

Data is sourced from goldprice.org (official market data) with Yahoo Finance as a backup. Prices are indicative market rates and may not reflect exact trading prices.

Can I see historical gold prices?

Yes, the interactive chart shows 30 days of historical gold price data. You can see daily price movements and trends.

Why is this better than asking AI?

AI chatbots cannot fetch real-time market data and have knowledge cutoffs. This tool provides live gold prices updated every minute with interactive charts.
