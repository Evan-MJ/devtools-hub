Frequently Asked Questions

Is the AI cover letter generator really free?

Yes, 100% free with no signup, no daily limit, and no hidden paywalls. Generate unlimited cover letters for any job application.

Can AI chatbots write a cover letter?

AI chatbots like ChatGPT can generate cover letter text, but they often produce generic, unstructured content that lacks personalization and ATS-friendly formatting. Our tool adds structure, tone options, and downloadable output so your letter is ready to submit.

What tone options are available?

Four tones: Professional (confident and achievement-focused), Enthusiastic (energetic and passionate), Formal (traditional business letter), and Concise (brief and direct).

Can I download my cover letter?

Yes. You can copy the cover letter to your clipboard or download it as a TXT file. The letter is formatted with proper spacing and structure, ready to paste into email or upload to job portals.

Is my information private?

Yes. Your data is sent securely to our API for generation and is not stored or logged. No signup or account needed.

What should I include in the skills section?

Include 3-6 key skills relevant to the job you are applying for. List technical skills, soft skills, or industry-specific expertise. The AI will weave these into the cover letter naturally.
