Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Use the "% of Number" mode. For example, to find 20% of 200: 20 ÷ 100 × 200 = 40.

How do I find the percentage increase?

Use the "% Change" mode. Enter the original value as "From" and the new value as "To". The calculator shows both the percentage change and whether it's an increase or decrease.

How do I reverse a percentage?

Use the "Reverse %" mode. If you know that 40 is 25% of some number, enter 40 and 25% to find the original number: 40 ÷ 25 × 100 = 160.
