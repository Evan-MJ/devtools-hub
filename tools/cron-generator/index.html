Build Expression

    Cron expressions have 5 fields: minute, hour, day of month, month, and day of week. Each field accepts specific values: * (any), numbers, ranges (1-5), or lists (1,15).

    Common patterns: 0 * * * * (every hour), 0 9 * * 1-5 (9 AM weekdays), */15 * * * * (every 15 minutes), 0 0 1 * * (midnight on 1st of month).

    Used in cron jobs, CI/CD pipelines, scheduled functions (AWS Lambda, Google Cloud Functions), and task schedulers. Most systems use UTC timezone—adjust accordingly for local execution times.
