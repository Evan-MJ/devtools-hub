Frequently Asked Questions

How is tip calculated?

Tip is calculated as: Bill Amount x Tip Percentage. For example, a $50 bill with 20% tip = $10 tip, total $60.

What's the standard tip percentage?

In the US, standard tip is 15-20% for sit-down restaurants. For counter service or takeaway, 10-15% is common. In Japan, tipping is not expected.

Should I tip before or after tax?

Most people calculate tip on the pre-tax amount. Check your receipt - if tax is listed separately, you can enter it in the "Exclude tax" field.

How do I split the bill?

Enter the number of people to split between. The calculator shows exactly how much each person pays, including their share of the tip.
