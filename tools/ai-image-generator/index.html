About This Tool

The AI Image Generator uses current state-of-the-art image models (GPT Image 2, Nano Banana, FLUX.1 Schnell, Seedream) to create images from text descriptions. Generation runs through Puter.js — a free, unlimited AI image API that needs no API key of your.

How It Works

  1. Enter a prompt — Describe the image you want in detail. Be specific about subjects, setting, style, lighting, and mood.
  2. Choose a model — GPT Image 2 is the best all-round default; Nano Banana Pro renders text inside images most reliably; FLUX.1 Schnell is the fastest.
  3. Select style and ratio — Pick from photorealistic, anime, oil painting, and more. Choose your preferred aspect ratio.
  4. Generate — Click the button and wait a few seconds for your image to appear.
  5. Download or iterate — Save your image as PNG or use it as a base for variations.

Prompt Writing Tips

  • Be specific: Instead of "a cat", say "a fluffy orange tabby cat sitting on a red velvet cushion"
  • Include details: Lighting ("golden hour sunlight"), mood ("peaceful and nostalgic"), composition ("rule of thirds")
  • Mention style: "photorealistic", "anime style", "oil painting", "digital art", "watercolor"
  • Add context: "professional product photography", "cinematic still", "magazine cover", "detailed illustration"

What AI Cannot Do (And We Can)

Large language models like ChatGPT and Claude can describe what an image should look like, but they cannot generate actual images. Even multimodal AI models that can analyze images cannot reliably create photorealistic images with specific compositions. This tool bridges that gap — you get the creative power of GPT Image 2 and FLUX running directly in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really free with no limits?

Yes. Puter.js operates on a "user-pays" model, so the model provider's cost is covered on your side rather than out of a shared quota. There is no cost to you, no subscription, and no credit card required.

What model should I use?

GPT Image 2 is the best default balance of quality, prompt following and reliability. Nano Banana Pro is the one to pick when your image needs legible text in it. FLUX.1 Schnell is the fastest, which makes it good for rapid iteration. For most users, GPT Image 2 is the right choice.

What aspect ratio should I use?

Use 1:1 (Square) for social media posts and general use. 16:9 is ideal for desktop wallpapers and presentations. 9:16 (Portrait) works best for Instagram Stories, TikTok, and mobile wallpapers. 3:2 is great for photography and prints.

Why does my image look different from my prompt?

AI image generation is probabilistic — the same prompt can produce different results each time. If the result doesn't match your vision, try: (1) making your prompt more specific, (2) adding style keywords like "photorealistic" or "anime style", (3) adjusting the style dropdown, or (4) generating again with the same prompt for a different result.

Can I use generated images commercially?

Images generated through OpenAI's GPT Image models are subject to OpenAI's usage policies. Generally, images you create are yours to use, but check the current OpenAI policies for the latest terms. FLUX.1 and Seedream models typically have more permissive licenses. For commercial use, consider crediting the model used.

Why does generation sometimes fail?

The AI services may have temporary capacity issues. If generation fails, wait a moment and try again. The error message will indicate if it's a service issue or a prompt problem (e.g., content policy violation).

Is my prompt stored or shared?

Your prompt is sent from your browser to the AI provider behind the model you selected, because that provider has to process it to produce the image. This site itself stores no prompts and no generated images, and adds no analytics on what you type. Providers' own retention terms apply to the generation request.
