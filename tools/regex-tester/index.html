Test Your Pattern

Regular expressions (regex) are patterns that match text. Common patterns: \d+ (digits), \w+ (word characters), [a-z]+ (lowercase letters), ^start (starts with), end$ (ends with).

Flags modify behavior: g (global - find all matches), i (case insensitive), m (multiline - ^/$ match line boundaries), s (dotall - . matches newlines).

Use capture groups (...) to extract parts. Backreferences \1 match previously captured text. Lookahead (?=...) matches without consuming.
