Frequently Asked Questions

Is this meme generator free?

Yes, completely free. No signup, no watermark, no hidden fees. All features are available without any limitations.

Can I upload my own images?

Yes, you can upload any image from your device. You can also choose from our built-in meme templates to get started quickly.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. All image processing happens entirely in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your images never leave your device.

Can I customize the text style?

Yes, you can adjust font size, text color, stroke (outline) color, and stroke width. The classic meme look is white text with a black outline, but you can choose any combination.

What image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, and most common image formats are supported for upload. The output is always PNG for maximum compatibility.
