What makes each tone different

Heartfelt

Emotional, sincere, focused on love and gratitude. Best for parents, partners, and close family. Uses phrases like "you mean the world to me" and "I am so grateful for you".

Funny

Light humor, gentle teasing, inside jokes. Best for friends and siblings. Avoids anything that could be taken the wrong way.

Romantic

Love, affection, intimacy. Best for partners, spouses, and that special someone. Includes lines about growing old together and being each other's person.

Professional

Respectful, appropriate for work. Best for colleagues, bosses, clients, and teachers. Avoids overly personal language.

Casual

Everyday friendly, conversational. Best for casual acquaintances, neighbors, and casual friends.

Poetic

Lyrical, rhythmic, sometimes rhymes. Best for the literary types in your life.
