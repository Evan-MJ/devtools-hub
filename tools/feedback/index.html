How to Report Problems

Found a bug or have a suggestion? Let us know!

What should I include in a bug report?

Please include: what you were trying to do, what went wrong, and any error messages you saw. Screenshots are helpful!

What happens after I submit?

We review all feedback and fix critical bugs as soon as possible. You can include your email if you want us to follow up.

Is feedback really free?

Yes! Reporting problems is completely free and requires no login.
