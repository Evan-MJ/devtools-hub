Frequently Asked Questions

Can AI chatbots do this?

No. AI chatbots cannot persistently store your notes or provide a real-time editing experience. A notepad is a tool, not something an AI conversation can replace.

Are my notes private?

Yes. All notes are stored locally in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server.

Will I lose my notes if I clear browser data?

Yes — clearing your browser's localStorage will delete your notes. Download important notes as .txt files to keep them safe.
